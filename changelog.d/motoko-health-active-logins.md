### Added
- Motoko now shows owner-only VPS health with CPU use, memory use, uptime, and sample age.
  The command palette opens the view through CPU, VPS, memory, uptime, and health search terms.
### Changed
- Owner sign-in permits at most three active browser logins. A fourth login requires explicit
  revocation of an old login from a device that is signed in. Passkey registration remains available.
